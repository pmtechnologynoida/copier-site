Frequently asked questions

Quick information about our photocopier, printer and printing services.

Do you provide photocopier machines on rent in Noida?

Yes, you can contact PM Technology to discuss photocopier rental requirements in Noida. Share your location, expected monthly volume, colour or black-and-white requirement and preferred contract duration for a quotation.

Can I request printer or photocopier repair?

Yes. Share the machine make/model, error code, a short description of the issue and your site location. Service availability and charges will be confirmed after reviewing the requirement.

What is included in an AMC?

AMC scope varies by machine and agreed plan. Ask for written details covering visits, labour, parts, consumables, exclusions, response expectations and applicable charges before confirming.

Do you provide toner cartridge refilling?

Contact us with your printer model and cartridge number. We can discuss refilling availability, compatibility and charges for your requirement.

Do you offer barcode, QR and product label printing?

We accept enquiries for barcode stickers, QR code labels, product labels, address labels, packaging labels, tags and customised sticker printing.

Which areas do you serve?

PM Technology is based in Sector 63, Noida. Contact us to confirm service availability for your exact location across Noida and the Delhi NCR area.
